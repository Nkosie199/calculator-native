Calculator Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 September 2026 · Publisher: Mynger Pty Ltd · Package: com.mynger.calculator

1. Information We Collect

  • Device data — device model, OS version, and anonymous crash/diagnostic logs

Note: Calculator does not require an account, does not collect calculation history off your device, and does not request any device permissions beyond what Android grants by default.

2. How We Use It

We use the information above to operate Calculator's core features, keep your account secure, respond to support requests, and diagnose crashes. We do not use it to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell it.

3. Data Sharing

We share data only with: (a) infrastructure providers who host Calculator's servers and storage under contract, (b) other users you explicitly interact with inside the app, and (c) law enforcement when legally compelled. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

4. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we must retain records to meet a legal obligation.

5. Security

We use industry-standard measures — encryption in transit, access controls, and regular review of who can reach production systems — to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

Calculator is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, email us using the contact details below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We'll update the effective date above whenever this policy changes, and — for material changes — notify you inside the app.
